How handoff works

1. You contact prospects (New → Contacted).   2. When one replies, move them to Replied.   3. Only then can you hand them over — the Handoff Queue locks New and Contacted leads out.   4. Handoff sets an assignee and a requirement note, and moves the lead to Requirement for the backend team.
